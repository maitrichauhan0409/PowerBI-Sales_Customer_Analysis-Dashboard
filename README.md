# 📊 Power BI Customer & Sales Analytics Dashboard

A comprehensive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze business performance, customer behavior, and sales metrics for data-driven decisions.

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

**Overview:**
  High-level KPIs and performance summary.
**Sales Analysis:** 
  Revenue trends, product performance, and regional sales.
**Churn Analysis:**
  Customer retention and churn tracking.
**CLV Analysis:** 
  Long-term customer value estimation.
**RFM Analysis:**
  Customer segmentation based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Power BI Desktop** (Visualization & DAX)
* **Power Query** (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
